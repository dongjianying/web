## 多元微分
多元微分求导问题

💡解答
 
完整解题过程
 
已知变换
$$
\begin{cases}
u=x-2y\\
v=x+ay
\end{cases}\quad(a\neq-2)
$$

z 是 u,v 的函数，u,v 又是 x,y 的函数，利用多元复合函数链式求导。
 
① 一阶偏导数
 
$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=1,\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=-2,\quad \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}=1,\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}=a
$$

$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}=z_u+z_v
$$

$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}=-2z_u+a z_v
$$
 
② 求二阶偏导数（z_u,z_v 仍然是 u,v 的函数，继续链式法则）
 
$$
\begin{aligned}
z_{xx}&=\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(z_u+z_v)=z_{uu}+2z_{uv}+z_{vv}\\
z_{xy}&=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(z_u+z_v)=-2z_{uu}+(a-2)z_{uv}+a z_{vv}\\
z_{yy}&=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(-2z_u+a z_v)=4z_{uu}-4a\,z_{uv}+a^2 z_{vv}
\end{aligned}
$$
 
③ 代入原方程 $\boldsymbol{6z_{xx}+z_{xy}-z_{yy}=0}$
 
$$
\begin{aligned}
&6\big(z_{uu}+2z_{uv}+z_{vv}\big)+\big[-2z_{uu}+(a-2)z_{uv}+a z_{vv}\big]-\big[4z_{uu}-4a z_{uv}+a^2 z_{vv}\big]=0\\
&\text{合并同类项：}\\
&(6-2-4)z_{uu}+\big[12+a-2+4a\big]z_{uv}+\big[6+a-a^2\big]z_{vv}=0\\
&\boldsymbol{(5a+10)z_{uv}+(-a^2+a+6)z_{vv}=0}
\end{aligned}
$$
 
④ 根据题意条件分析
 
要求方程化简为 $\boldsymbol{\dfrac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v}=0}$，意味着：
 
$$
1. z_{vv} 的系数必须为 0；
2. z_{uv} 的系数不能为 0。
$$
 
令
$$
-a^2+a+6=0 \implies a^2-a-6=0 \implies (a-3)(a+2)=0

解得 a=3 或 a=-2，题目给出 a\neq-2。
 
验证：a=3 时，z_{uv} 系数 5a+10=25\neq0，符合条件。
$$
答案
$$
\boldsymbol{a=3}
$$
 
 
关键提醒（你刚才疑惑的二阶偏导要点）
$$
z_u=\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial u}、z_v=\dfrac{\partial z}{\partial v} 依旧是 u,v 的二元函数，再次对 x/y 求导时，必须继续使用链式法则，不要漏掉混合偏导 z_{uv}！
$$

我把二阶偏导的计算拆成最底层的步骤讲清楚，核心逻辑是：
一阶偏导 ∂z/∂u、∂z/∂v 本身仍然是 u、v 的函数，再对 x/y 求导时，必须再套一次链式法则，和求一阶偏导的规则完全一样。
 
 
先记住通用公式（灵魂）
 
对于任意一个关于 u、v 的函数 f(u,v)，而 u,v 又是 x,y 的函数，必有：
$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial u}\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial v}\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial u}\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial f}{\partial v}\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}
$$

求二阶偏导时，我们就把 f 分别换成 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}$ 和 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial v} $即可。
 
已知：$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=1,\ \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=-2,\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}=1,\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial y}=a$
 
 
 
一、计算 $\boldsymbol{\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}}$
 
第一步先展开：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)
$$
 
① 算第一项：$\boldsymbol{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)}$
 
令 $f = \frac{\partial z}{\partial u}$，代入通用链式公式：
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial u}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial}{\partial v}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \\
&= \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} \cdot 1 + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v} \cdot 1 \\
&= \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v}
\end{align*}
$$
 
② 算第二项：$\boldsymbol{\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)}$
 
令 $f = \frac{\partial z}{\partial v}$，同理：
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial u}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial}{\partial v}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial x} \\
&= \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u} \cdot 1 + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2} \cdot 1 \\
&= \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
\end{align*}
$$
 
③ 合并两项
 
二阶混合偏导连续时，$\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v} = \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u}$，因此：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + 2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v} + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
$$

 
 
 
二、计算 $\boldsymbol{\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}}$（对 y 求导）
 
同样展开：
$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right) = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)
$$
 
① 算第一项：$\boldsymbol{\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)}$
 
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial u}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial}{\partial v}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \\
&= \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} \cdot (-2) + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v} \cdot a \\
&= -2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v}
\end{align*}
$$
 
② 算第二项：$\boldsymbol{\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)}$
 
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)
&= \frac{\partial}{\partial u}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial}{\partial v}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)\cdot\frac{\partial v}{\partial y} \\
&= \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u} \cdot (-2) + \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2} \cdot a \\
&= -2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
\end{align*}
$$
 
③ 合并同类项
 
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y}
&= \left(-2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v}\right) + \left(-2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}\right) \\
&= -2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + (a-2)\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
\end{align*}
$$
 
 
 
三、计算 $\boldsymbol{\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}}$
 
先展开：

$$
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(-2\frac{\partial z}{\partial u} + a\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right) = -2\cdot\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right) + a\cdot\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)
$$
 
上面我们已经算出了$\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\right) 和 \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)$，直接代入：
 
$$
\begin{align*}
\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}
&= -2\left(-2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v}\right) + a\left(-2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}\right) \\
&= 4\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} - 2a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v} - 2a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v\partial u} + a^2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2} \\
&= 4\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u^2} - 4a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial u\partial v} + a^2\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial v^2}
\end{align*}
$$
